## Ingredients

- 150 g quinoa
- 600 ml milk
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 pinch salt
- 100 g Greek yogurt
- 30 g pecans, toasted and chopped

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa well in a fine sieve.
2. Put it in a heavy pan with the milk, cinnamon stick and salt.
3. Bring to a bare simmer and cook uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring every few minutes so it doesn't catch. It should be loose and creamy — it thickens a lot as it cools.
4. Fish out the cinnamon stick and stir through the honey and vanilla.
5. Serve warm, each portion topped with a spoon of Greek yogurt and the pecans.

## Notes

Cold from the fridge it sets firm. Loosen it with a splash of milk and it's
back to how it was.
