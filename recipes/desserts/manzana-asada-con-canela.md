## Ingredients

- 1 apple
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 15 g almonds

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan.
2. Core the apple and cut it into thick wedges.
3. Dust with the cinnamon and bake for 15 minutes until soft at the edges.
4. Serve with the almonds.

## Notes
