## Ingredients

- 500 g Greek yogurt, full fat and cold
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 lemon, zested
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 300 g mixed berries
- 40 g pistachios, chopped
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Put the cold yogurt, honey, vanilla, lemon zest and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk hard for 2 minutes — by hand or with electric beaters. It loosens first, then thickens and lightens noticeably. Stop there.
3. Crush a third of the berries with a fork to make a rough compote.
4. Layer the whipped yogurt and crushed berries into four glasses, finishing with yogurt.
5. Top with the whole berries and pistachios.

## Notes

Assemble no more than an hour ahead — the whipped texture slackens after that.
The base mixture on its own keeps 2 days and is excellent on the
[protein pancakes](/recipes/breakfast/greek-yogurt-protein-pancakes/).
