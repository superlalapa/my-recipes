## Ingredients

- 200 ml olive oil, plus more for the tin
- 250 g caster sugar
- 3 eggs
- 250 g plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 150 ml whole milk
- 2 lemons, zested
- 3 tbsp lemon juice

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 170°C fan. Oil a 23 cm round tin and line the base.
2. Whisk the sugar and eggs for 3 minutes, until pale and thickened.
3. Pour in the olive oil in a slow stream, whisking as you go.
4. Fold in the flour, baking powder and salt in two additions.
5. Stir through the milk, lemon zest and juice. The batter will be loose — that's right.
6. Bake for 40–45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean and the top is deep gold.
7. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes before turning out.

## Notes

Better on day two. Keep it covered at room temperature, not in the fridge.
