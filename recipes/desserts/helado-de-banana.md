## Ingredients

- 1 banana, frozen in chunks
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 100 ml light or almond milk

## Method

1. Freeze the banana in chunks the night before — this is the whole trick.
2. Blend the frozen chunks with the cocoa and milk.
3. It will look crumbly, then suddenly turn smooth. Stop there.
4. Eat immediately, or freeze for 30 minutes for a firmer scoop.

## Notes

Weeks 3, 9 and 11. Adding too much milk at the start gives you a smoothie
rather than ice cream.
