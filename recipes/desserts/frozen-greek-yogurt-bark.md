## Ingredients

- 500 g Greek yogurt, full fat
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 150 g raspberries
- 100 g blueberries
- 40 g pistachios, roughly chopped
- 20 g dark chocolate, shaved

## Method

1. Line a baking tray with parchment, leaving an overhang at the sides.
2. Stir the honey and vanilla through the yogurt. Use full fat — low fat freezes hard and icy.
3. Spread it over the tray to about 1 cm thick. Don't go thinner or it shatters into dust.
4. Scatter over the berries, pressing them in slightly, then the pistachios and chocolate.
5. Freeze for at least 4 hours, or overnight.
6. Lift out using the parchment and break into shards with your hands.

## Notes

Keeps a month in a sealed container in the freezer. Let it sit for 2 minutes
before eating — straight from the freezer it's too hard on the teeth.
