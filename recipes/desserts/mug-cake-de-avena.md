## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp oat flour
- 1/2 tbsp stevia, or 1 tbsp Lakanto
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 banana, mashed
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 splash plant milk
- 1 egg

## Method

1. Mix everything together in a mug until no dry flour remains.
2. Microwave for 2 minutes.
3. Let it stand a minute before eating — it firms up as it cools.

## Notes

Weeks 9, 11 and 13. If it overflows, use a bigger mug rather than less batter.
