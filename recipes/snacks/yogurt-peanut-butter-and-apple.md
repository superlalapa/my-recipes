## Ingredients

- 1/3 Greek yogurt
- 1/3 peanut butter
- 1 apple

## Method

1. Stir the peanut butter through the Greek yogurt until streak-free.
2. Slice the apple and dip.

## Notes

The source sheet gives the yogurt and peanut butter as "1/3" with no unit. 1/3 cup of yogurt with 1 tbsp of peanut butter is the sensible reading.
