## Ingredients

- 1 slice wholemeal bread
- 1 tomato, finely diced
- 30 g fresh cheese or light mozzarella, diced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 4 basil leaves
- 1 tsp olive oil

## Method

1. Toast the bread.
2. Mix the diced tomato and cheese with the oregano and oil.
3. Pile it onto the toast and tear the basil over.

## Notes

Appears in six of the eight weeks. Dice rather than slice — it stays on the
toast.
