## Ingredients

- 600 g cauliflower rice
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested
- 1 handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 pinch black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oil in your widest frying pan over medium-high. Surface area is the whole game here.
2. Add the cauliflower rice and spread it into an even layer. Leave it undisturbed for 3 minutes so it toasts rather than steams.
3. Stir once, spread it out again, and give it another 3 minutes.
4. Push it to one side, add the garlic to the clear space with a little more oil, and cook 45 seconds until fragrant.
5. Toss everything together with the salt and pepper and cook 2 minutes more.
6. Off the heat, stir through the lemon zest and parsley.

## Notes

If it releases a lot of water, tip the pan and spoon it off rather than
cooking it away — you'll overcook the cauliflower waiting.
