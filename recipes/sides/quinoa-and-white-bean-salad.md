## Ingredients

- 200 g quinoa
- 400 ml vegetable stock
- 400 g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 100 g spinach, finely shredded
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 handful mint, chopped
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly, then simmer it covered in the stock for 15 minutes. Rest 5 minutes off the heat, then fork it through and spread it on a tray to cool quickly.
2. Soak the diced red onion in the vinegar for 10 minutes. This takes the raw bite out of it.
3. Whisk the oil, mustard and salt into the onion and vinegar to make the dressing.
4. Combine the cooled quinoa, beans, tomatoes, cucumber and shredded spinach.
5. Add the garlic and dressing and turn everything over gently — the beans break up if you're rough.
6. Stir through the mint just before serving.

## Notes

Keeps 4 days. Add the mint fresh each time rather than all at once, or it goes
dark and loses its lift.
