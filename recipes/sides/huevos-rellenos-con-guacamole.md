## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1/2 avocado
- 2 tbsp sweetcorn
- 1 tomato, finely diced
- 1 tbsp onion, finely diced
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, then cool them under cold running water and peel.
2. Halve them and lift out the yolks.
3. Mash the yolks with the avocado, lime juice and salt.
4. Fold in the sweetcorn, tomato and onion.
5. Spoon the mixture back into the whites.

## Notes

Week 12 dinner. Week 3 fills them with turkey ham or shredded chicken instead.
