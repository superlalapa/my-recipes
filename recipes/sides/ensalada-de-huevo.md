## Ingredients

- 1 egg, hard-boiled and sliced
- 3 cups mixed green leaves
- 2 tbsp sweetcorn
- 1/2 avocado
- 1 tomato, diced
- 30 g fresh cheese, diced

## Method

1. Arrange the leaves in a bowl.
2. Add the sweetcorn, avocado, tomato and cheese.
3. Lay the egg slices over the top.
4. Dress with olive oil and lemon.

## Notes

Weeks 9, 11 and 13. Served with a cup of infusion and lemon juice.
