## Ingredients

- 500 g spinach
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 pinch flaky salt
- 1 pinch nutmeg, grated

## Method

1. Warm the oil and garlic together in a cold pan over medium-low heat. Starting cold draws the flavour out without burning it.
2. When the garlic is pale gold — about 3 minutes — turn the heat up.
3. Add the spinach in three batches, waiting for each to collapse before adding the next.
4. Cook for 1 minute more, then tip the lot into a sieve and press gently to lose the excess water.
5. Return to the pan off the heat and toss with lemon juice, salt and a scrape of nutmeg.

## Notes

500 g of raw spinach cooks down to almost nothing — it looks absurd in the pan
and serves four. Don't be tempted to halve it.
