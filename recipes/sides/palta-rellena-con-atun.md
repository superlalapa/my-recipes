## Ingredients

- 1/2 avocado
- 1/2 tin tuna, drained
- 1 cup vegetables of your choice, finely diced
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Scoop out a little of the avocado flesh to widen the hollow, and mash it with the tuna.
2. Fold the diced vegetables and lemon juice through.
3. Pile the mixture back into the avocado half.

## Notes

Weeks 4, 9, 10, 12 and 14. Serve with a cup of infusion and lemon juice.
