## Ingredients

- 120 g chicken or prawns, cooked
- 3 cups mixed green leaves
- 3 tbsp sweetcorn
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1/2 avocado
- 1/2 cup quinoa or rice, cooked

## Method

1. Pile the leaves into a wide bowl.
2. Add the sweetcorn, tomato and avocado.
3. Slice the chicken or leave the prawns whole, and lay them over the top.
4. Scatter the sesame seeds and serve the quinoa or rice alongside.

## Notes

The quinoa is a weeks 13–14 addition; earlier weeks leave it out. Rinse tinned
sweetcorn well.
