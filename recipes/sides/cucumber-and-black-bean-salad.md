## Ingredients

- 400 g black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1/3 cup red onion, finely diced
- 1/2 cup coriander, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp feta, crumbled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/8 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper, freshly cracked

## Method

1. Soak the diced red onion in ice water for 5 minutes to take the raw edge off, then drain.
2. Rinse and drain the black beans thoroughly.
3. Dice the cucumber to roughly the size of the beans — it eats better when everything is the same scale.
4. Whisk the oil, vinegar, cumin, garlic powder, salt and pepper together.
5. Combine the beans, cucumber, onion, coriander and feta in a large bowl and fold the dressing through gently.
6. Leave it 10 minutes before serving so the beans take on the dressing.

## Notes

The feta is optional — leave it out and the salad is vegan. Keeps 4–5 days.
