## Ingredients

- 500 g tenderstem broccoli
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 50 g flaked almonds
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 pinch flaky salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C fan.
2. Toss the broccoli with 2 tbsp of the oil and a good pinch of salt.
3. Spread it on a hot tray in a single layer and roast for 12 minutes, until the tips are properly blackened.
4. Meanwhile, toast the almonds in a dry pan until golden, then set aside.
5. Toss the hot broccoli with the remaining oil, chilli, lemon zest and juice.
6. Scatter the almonds over just before serving so they stay crisp.
