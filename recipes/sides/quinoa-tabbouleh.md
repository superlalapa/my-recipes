## Ingredients

- 1 cup quinoa, uncooked
- 1.75 cups water
- 1 large lemon, juiced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 large tomato, diced small
- 1 large cucumber, diced small
- 1 bunch curly parsley, finely chopped

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly in a fine sieve.
2. Bring it to the boil with the water, covered, then drop to low and simmer for 15 minutes until the water is absorbed.
3. Cool it completely. Spreading it on a baking sheet and refrigerating for 30 minutes gets you there faster — warm quinoa wilts the parsley.
4. Whisk the lemon juice, garlic, olive oil and salt into a dressing.
5. Combine the diced tomato, cucumber and chopped parsley in a large bowl.
6. Add the cooled quinoa, pour over the dressing, and stir until everything is coated.

## Notes

Parsley is the main ingredient here, not a garnish — a whole bunch is correct.
Keeps 4 days; stir before serving as the dressing settles.
