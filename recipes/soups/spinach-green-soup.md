## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 2.5 cups spinach
- 1 bouillon cube
- 1/4 avocado
- 6–8 oz chicken breast or shrimp
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Bring the water to the boil and stir in the bouillon cube until dissolved.
2. Add the vegetables and simmer for 8–10 minutes, until tender but still bright.
3. Blend until completely smooth. A jug blender gives a silkier result than a stick blender.
4. Add the avocado and blend again — this is what makes it creamy without any cream.
5. Stir in the cooked chicken or shrimp and warm through for 2 minutes.
6. Season and serve.

## Notes

Spinach needs about 2 minutes, not 8 — overcook it and both the colour and the flavour go flat.
