## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 1/2 cup cauliflower
- 1 bouillon cube
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1/2 avocado
- 6–8 oz chicken breast or shrimp
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin, turmeric and paprika

## Method

1. Bring the water to the boil and stir in the bouillon cube until dissolved.
2. Add the cauliflower and simmer for 10 minutes, until a knife goes through easily.
3. Stir in the spinach and cook 1 minute more — just long enough to collapse it.
4. Add the spices and blend until completely smooth.
5. Add the avocado and blend again until creamy.
6. Stir in the cooked chicken or shrimp and warm through.
7. Season and serve.

## Notes

The source sheet reads "2/4 cup" of cauliflower, written here as 1/2 cup. The other green soups use 1.5–2.5 cups of vegetable, so this is likely a typo — use more if the soup comes out thin.

The spice line in the sheet reads "1/2 teaspoon ground cumin, turmeric, paprika" — 
read here as 1/2 tsp between the three. Use 1/2 tsp of each if you want it bolder.
