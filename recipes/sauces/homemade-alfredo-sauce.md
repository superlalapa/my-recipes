Makes about 2 cups — enough for 400–450 g of pasta.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1/2 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 cup Parmesan, freshly grated

## Method

1. Melt the butter, cream and cream cheese together in a medium saucepan over medium heat, whisking as it comes together.
2. Whisk in the minced garlic, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
3. Add the Parmesan a handful at a time, whisking between additions so it melts smoothly instead of clumping.
4. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, still whisking, until it coats a spoon.
5. Toss with cooked fettuccine and serve immediately.

## Notes

Grate the Parmesan yourself. Canned Parmesan makes the sauce grainy and
pre-shredded cheese turns it clumpy.

To thicken it further, whisk 2 tsp of cornstarch into 1 tbsp of water and stir
that in.
