## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup fat-free milk
- 1 cup frozen raspberries
- 1/4 cup blueberries
- 1/2 orange
- 2 tbsp protein powder
- 2 tbsp unflavored protein powder

## Method

1. Put the liquid base into the blender first — liquid at the bottom stops the blades cavitating.
2. Add the fruit, yogurt or protein, and any nuts or seeds.
3. Add anything frozen, and ice, last so it sits on top.
4. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds, until completely smooth.
5. Loosen with a splash more liquid if it's thicker than you want.

## Notes

The source sheet lists protein powder in both the healthy-fat and lean-protein slots. Going by the [Red Smoothie](/recipes/smoothies/red-smoothie/), the fat slot was probably meant to be flaxseed — swap it in if you want the fibre.
