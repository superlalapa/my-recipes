## Ingredients

- 1 cup mixed red berries
- 250 ml almond or light milk
- 1 scoop protein powder

## Method

1. Put the milk in the blender first.
2. Add the berries and the protein powder.
3. Blend for 30 seconds until smooth.

## Notes

Appears in seven of the eight weeks — every week except week 9. The protein powder is an alternative to the
milk in some weeks, not always an addition.
