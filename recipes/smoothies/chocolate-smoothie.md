## Ingredients

- 1 cup fat-free milk
- 1 banana
- 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 tsp Lakanto sweetener
- 5 raw almonds
- 2 tbsp whey protein

## Method

1. Put the liquid base into the blender first — liquid at the bottom stops the blades cavitating.
2. Add the fruit, yogurt or protein, and any nuts or seeds.
3. Add anything frozen, and ice, last so it sits on top.
4. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds, until completely smooth.
5. Loosen with a splash more liquid if it's thicker than you want.
