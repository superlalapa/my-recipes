## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 tbsp mixed seeds
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Mix the egg, oats, seeds and salt in a mug or small bowl.
2. Microwave for 2 minutes, or cook covered in a small non-stick pan over low heat until set.
3. Turn out and slice.

## Notes

The plan pairs it with a fried egg or 2 tbsp of shredded chicken, vegetables
and 1/4 avocado.
