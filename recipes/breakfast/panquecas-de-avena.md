## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp rolled oats
- 1/2 banana
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 1 splash light milk

## Method

1. Blend the oats, banana and egg until smooth.
2. Add the vanilla, cinnamon and a splash of milk if the batter is stiff. Stevia if you want it sweeter.
3. Cook spoonfuls in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat, 2–3 minutes a side.

## Notes

Serve with a cup of strawberries, or with chopped banana and a spoon of honey
as in weeks 13 and 14.
