## Ingredients

- 200 g Greek yogurt, full fat
- 2 eggs
- 80 g rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 tsp butter, for the pan

## Method

1. Blitz the oats in a blender until they look like coarse flour.
2. Add everything else except the butter and blend again until smooth. The batter should be thick enough to mound on a spoon.
3. Let it stand for 5 minutes — the oats drink up liquid and the baking powder gets going.
4. Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Too hot and the outside burns before the middle sets.
5. Spoon in rounds of batter and cook for 2–3 minutes, until bubbles break the surface and stay open.
6. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes more.

## Notes

Serve with more Greek yogurt and berries rather than syrup — keeps the sugar
down and the protein up. Batter holds overnight in the fridge.
