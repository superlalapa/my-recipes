## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 400 g tinned plum tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- 100 g feta
- 1 handful flat-leaf parsley

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a heavy frying pan and cook the onion and pepper gently for 12 minutes, until soft and sweet.
2. Add the garlic and spices. Stir for a minute, until fragrant.
3. Crush in the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, until thick enough to hold a channel when you drag a spoon through it.
4. Make four wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each.
5. Cover and cook for 6–8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still run.
6. Crumble over the feta, scatter the parsley, and take the pan straight to the table.

## Notes

Bread is not optional. Sourdough, toasted, for scooping.
