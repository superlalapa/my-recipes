## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 1 slice turkey ham, or 2 tbsp shredded chicken
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Blend the eggs, spinach and salt until completely smooth and bright green.
2. Pour a thin layer into a hot non-stick pan and swirl to coat.
3. Cook 1–2 minutes until set, then flip briefly.
4. Fill with the turkey ham or shredded chicken and roll up.

## Notes

Makes two thin crêpes, or one thick one.
