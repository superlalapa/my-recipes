## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 1 slice turkey ham, or 2 tbsp shredded chicken
- 1 slice wholemeal toast

## Method

1. Wilt the spinach in a hot pan for a minute.
2. Beat the eggs and pour them over.
3. Stir gently until softly scrambled.
4. Fold the turkey ham or chicken through and serve with the toast.

## Notes

Week 14 dinner. A quarter of an avocado on the toast rounds it out.
