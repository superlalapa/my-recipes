## Ingredients

- 1 pita bread
- 1 slice turkey ham
- 1 slice cheese
- 1 handful spinach

## Method

1. Toast the pita until it firms up.
2. Split it and layer in the turkey ham, cheese and spinach.
3. Return it to the heat for a minute so the cheese softens.

## Notes
