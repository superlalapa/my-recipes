## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 2 slices potato
- 1 cup vegetables of your choice
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Slice the potato thinly and cook it gently in the oil until tender, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the vegetables and cook until softened.
3. Beat the eggs with the salt and pour them in.
4. Cook over low heat until almost set, then flip and give it another minute.

## Notes

Weeks 13 and 14 use three slices of potato. Serve with a cup of infusion and
the juice of a lemon.
