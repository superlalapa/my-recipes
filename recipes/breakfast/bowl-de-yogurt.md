## Ingredients

- 160 ml low-fat high-protein yogurt
- 3 strawberries, or 1 peach
- 1/2 tbsp chia seeds
- 1/2 banana

## Method

1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
2. Slice the fruit over it.
3. Scatter the chia seeds on top and eat straight away, or leave it 10 minutes if you prefer the chia softened.

## Notes

Weeks 3, 4, 9, 11 and 13 all use a version of this. Half a cup of cherries or
raspberries works in place of the strawberries.
