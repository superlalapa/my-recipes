## Ingredients

- 150 g quinoa
- 350 ml milk
- 100 ml water
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 pinch salt
- 150 g Greek yogurt
- 100 g blueberries
- 30 g flaked almonds, toasted

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa in a fine sieve for a good 30 seconds. Skip this and it tastes soapy.
2. Put it in a pan with the milk, water, cinnamon and salt.
3. Bring to a bare simmer, then cover and cook gently for 15 minutes.
4. Turn off the heat and leave it covered for another 5 minutes — the grains finish swelling and the little tails uncurl.
5. Stir through the maple syrup and loosen with a splash more milk if it's stiffer than you want.
6. Spoon into bowls and top with Greek yogurt, blueberries and almonds.

## Notes

Doubles well and reheats with a splash of milk. Cook it the night before and
breakfast is 90 seconds in the microwave.
