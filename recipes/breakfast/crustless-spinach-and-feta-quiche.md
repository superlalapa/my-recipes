## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs
- 1/2 cup milk, or unsweetened almond milk
- 2 cups fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup feta, crumbled
- 1/4 cup parmesan, grated, or shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 small onion, finely diced
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 pinch black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C / 375°F. Lightly grease a 9-inch pie dish or round baking pan with olive oil.
2. Whisk the eggs, milk, garlic powder, salt and pepper together until well combined.
3. Spread the chopped spinach, diced onion and feta evenly across the bottom of the dish.
4. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables and cheese, then sprinkle the parmesan across the top.
5. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until the centre is set and the edges are lightly golden.
6. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

The onion is optional.

It reheats exceptionally well — keep slices in the fridge for up to 4 days and
microwave for 45–60 seconds.
