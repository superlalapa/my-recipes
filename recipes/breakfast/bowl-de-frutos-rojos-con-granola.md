## Ingredients

- 2 cups mixed berries
- 1 banana
- 15 g granola
- 1 egg, or 2 slices turkey ham

## Method

1. Slice the banana into a bowl with the berries.
2. Scatter the granola over the top.
3. Serve with a boiled or fried egg, or two rolled slices of turkey ham, on the side.

## Notes

Week 13 also serves 50 g of granola on its own as an afternoon snack.
