## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp rolled oats
- 250 ml almond milk
- 1/2 cup blueberries, or 1 apple
- 15 g almonds
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon

## Method

1. Put the oats and almond milk in a small pan over medium heat.
2. Simmer for 6–8 minutes, stirring, until it thickens.
3. Pour into a bowl and top with the fruit and almonds.
4. Add cinnamon to taste.

## Notes

A banana instead of the berries is the week 13 variation.
