## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp rolled oats
- 2 eggs
- 50 g fresh cheese or light mozzarella
- 1 cup vegetables of your choice
- 1 tbsp mustard or a light dressing

## Method

1. Blend the oats and eggs into a smooth batter and let it stand 5 minutes.
2. Pour a thin layer into a hot non-stick pan and cook 2 minutes a side.
3. Fill with the cheese and vegetables.
4. Add the mustard or dressing and fold over.

## Notes

From the week 4 Saturday dinner. Good with a glass of strawberry juice.
