Roughly 15 g of protein per cup and nothing you can't eat cold out of the fridge.

## Ingredients

- 6 slices ham
- 8 eggs
- 150 g spinach
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 60 g Greek yogurt
- 40 g feta, crumbled
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 pinch black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C fan and lightly oil a 6-hole muffin tin.
2. Press a slice of ham into each hole so it forms a cup. The edges will ruffle — that's what you want.
3. Warm the oil in a pan and cook the garlic for 30 seconds, until it smells sweet but hasn't coloured.
4. Add the spinach a handful at a time and cook until fully wilted, about 3 minutes. Tip it into a sieve and press hard — wet spinach makes soggy cups.
5. Whisk the eggs with the Greek yogurt and pepper until completely smooth.
6. Divide the spinach between the ham cups, pour the egg over, and top with feta.
7. Bake for 18–20 minutes, until puffed and just set in the middle.
8. Cool for 5 minutes before lifting them out, or they'll tear.

## Notes

Keeps 4 days in the fridge. Don't add salt — the ham and feta bring plenty.
