## Ingredients

- 200 g minced beef
- 1 egg
- 1 cup vegetables, grated
- 1 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Mix the beef, egg, grated vegetables, oats and salt with your hands until it holds together.
2. Shape into two patties and rest them 5 minutes.
3. Cook in a hot dry pan for 4–5 minutes a side.
4. Serve with half a plate of vegetables, strawberries, 1/2 avocado and 2 tbsp of sweetcorn.

## Notes
