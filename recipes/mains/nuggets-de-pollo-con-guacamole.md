## Ingredients

- 150 g chicken nuggets
- 1/2 avocado
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 tbsp onion, finely diced
- 2 cups vegetables of your choice
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Cook the nuggets in the air fryer according to the packet — no oil needed.
2. Mash the avocado with the lime juice, onion and salt to make the guacamole.
3. Serve the nuggets with the guacamole and the vegetables.

## Notes

Weeks 3, 11, 12, 13 and 14. Week 13 adds three slices of oven-baked potato;
