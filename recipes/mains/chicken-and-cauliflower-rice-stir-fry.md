The trick is heat and patience: cauliflower rice steams itself soggy if you
crowd the pan or keep stirring.

## Ingredients

- 500 g chicken breast, sliced thin
- 500 g cauliflower rice
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 20 g ginger, grated
- 100 g spinach
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp white pepper

## Method

1. Get a large wok or frying pan properly hot before anything goes in.
2. Add the neutral oil and the chicken in a single layer. Leave it alone for 2 minutes to colour, then stir and cook 2 minutes more. Remove it.
3. Pour in the beaten egg, let it set for 30 seconds, then scramble it roughly and lift it out with the chicken.
4. Add the garlic and ginger to the dry-ish pan for 30 seconds.
5. Tip in the cauliflower rice, spread it out, and leave it for 2 minutes before stirring. Repeat twice — you want a bit of browning, not steam.
6. Return the chicken and egg, add the spinach and soy sauce, and toss until the spinach collapses.
7. Off the heat, stir through the sesame oil, white pepper and spring onions.

## Notes

Frozen cauliflower rice works, but cook it from frozen and give it an extra
few minutes to drive off the water.
