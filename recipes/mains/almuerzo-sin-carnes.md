## Ingredients

- 1 cup lentils, chickpeas or beans, cooked
- 1/2 avocado
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 onion, finely sliced
- 2 cups lettuce
- 1 egg

## Method

1. Warm the pulses through, or use them cold from the fridge.
2. Combine the tomato, onion and lettuce.
3. Add the pulses and the avocado.
4. Fry the egg and set it on top.

## Notes

Appears in weeks 3, 9 and 12. A glass of lemonade alongside.
