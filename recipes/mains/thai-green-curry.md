Fry the paste properly before the coconut milk goes in — that one step is the
difference between a flat curry and a fragrant one.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp green curry paste
- 400 ml coconut milk, full fat
- 500 g chicken thigh, sliced
- 200 g green beans, trimmed
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp palm sugar
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 handful Thai basil
- 2 tbsp coconut oil

## Method

1. Heat the coconut oil in a wide pan over medium heat.
2. Fry the curry paste for 2–3 minutes, until it smells sweet rather than raw.
3. Pour in the thick top layer of the coconut milk and let it split and sizzle.
4. Add the chicken and turn it through the paste until sealed.
5. Add the rest of the coconut milk, the fish sauce and the sugar. Simmer 10 minutes.
6. Stir in the beans and pepper. Cook 5 minutes more — they should keep some bite.
7. Off the heat, add the lime juice and Thai basil. Taste and adjust.

## Notes

Vegetarian version: swap the chicken for firm tofu and the fish sauce for light
soy plus a pinch of salt. Add the tofu at step 5 so it doesn't break up.
