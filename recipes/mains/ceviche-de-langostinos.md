## Ingredients

- 120 g prawns, cooked
- 1/2 avocado, cubed
- 1/2 red pepper, diced
- 1/2 onion, finely sliced
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 slice mango, diced
- 2 slices sweet potato

## Method

1. Soak the sliced onion in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain.
2. Combine the prawns, pepper and onion with the lime juice and leave 5 minutes.
3. Fold in the avocado and mango gently so the cubes hold their shape.
4. Serve with the sweet potato, or with chips made from a small pita.

## Notes

Week 3 lunch. The prawns are already cooked — the lime is for flavour, not for
curing.
