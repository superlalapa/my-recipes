## Ingredients

- 200 g quinoa
- 400 ml vegetable stock
- 400 g black beans, drained and rinsed
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 limes
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 150 g Greek yogurt
- 1 handful coriander
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa well, then simmer it in the stock, covered, for 15 minutes. Rest it off the heat for 5 minutes and fork it through.
2. Meanwhile, warm 1 tbsp of the oil and cook the red onion for 4 minutes until it softens.
3. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika. Stir for 1 minute — the spices need the heat to open up.
4. Tip in the black beans with a splash of water and warm through for 5 minutes, crushing a few against the side of the pan to thicken things.
5. Stir the juice of 1 lime and the remaining oil through the quinoa, and season.
6. Build the bowls: quinoa, beans, avocado, a spoon of Greek yogurt, coriander, and the second lime cut into wedges.

## Notes

The beans and quinoa both keep 4 days. Store the avocado component uncut.
