## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup pasta
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 1/2 avocado
- 1/2 cup courgette, cut into ribbons or grated
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 30 g fresh cheese
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Boil the pasta.
2. Meanwhile blend the spinach, avocado and olive oil into a smooth sauce, loosening it with a little pasta water.
3. Cook the courgette ribbons and mushrooms in a hot pan for 4 minutes.
4. Drain the pasta and toss it with the sauce and the vegetables.
5. Top with the tomato and the fresh cheese.

## Notes

Weeks 11 and 13. Week 13 scales up to 2 cups of pasta with 120 g of chicken.
