## Ingredients

- 600 g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 300 g long-grain rice
- 700 ml chicken stock, hot
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 200 g spinach
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon
- 1 tsp salt

## Method

1. Season the chicken with the paprika and half the salt.
2. Heat the oil in a wide, lidded pan over medium-high and brown the thighs well on both sides, about 8 minutes total. Lift them out — they don't need to be cooked through yet.
3. Turn the heat down. Cook the onion in the chicken fat for 5 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute more.
4. Stir in the rice and turn it through the fat until every grain is glossy.
5. Pour in the hot stock, add the rest of the salt, and sit the chicken back on top.
6. Cover and cook on the lowest heat for 18 minutes. Don't lift the lid.
7. Off the heat, pile the spinach on top, put the lid straight back on, and leave 5 minutes — the residual steam wilts it perfectly.
8. Fork the spinach through the rice and finish with a hard squeeze of lemon.

## Notes

Brown rice works but needs about 40 minutes and 900 ml of stock.
