## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup beans or chickpeas, cooked
- 2 cups mixed leaves
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 avocado
- 3 tbsp sweetcorn
- 1 egg, hard-boiled and sliced
- 1/2 tbsp sesame seeds

## Method

1. Lay the leaves in the base of a wide bowl.
2. Arrange the beans, tomato, avocado and sweetcorn in sections on top.
3. Add the sliced egg.
4. Finish with the sesame seeds.

## Notes

Weeks 13 and 14 swap the egg for 120 g of minced beef; week 3 uses 120 g of
grilled chicken. Leftover beans from the day before are the point.
