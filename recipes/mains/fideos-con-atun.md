## Ingredients

- 1 cup pasta
- 1/2 cup sweetcorn
- 1/2 tin tuna, drained
- 1/2 onion, finely diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 avocado
- 1 splash olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Method

1. Boil the pasta, adding the sweetcorn for the last two minutes.
2. Drain and dress with the olive oil and oregano while still hot.
3. Fold through the tuna, onion and tomato.
4. Serve with the avocado.

## Notes

Weeks 4 and 9. Good cold the next day.
