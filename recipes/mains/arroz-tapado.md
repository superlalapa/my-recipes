## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup brown rice, cooked
- 100 g minced beef
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tomato, diced
- 1/4 avocado
- 1 pinch salt

## Method

1. Brown the minced beef in a dry pan, breaking it up, then season.
2. Stir the diced tomato through and cook 2 minutes more.
3. Press half the rice into a small bowl or ring mould.
4. Add the beef in a layer, then top with the remaining rice and press down firmly.
5. Turn out onto a plate.
6. Serve with a fried egg on top and the avocado alongside.
