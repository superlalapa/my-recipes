## Ingredients

- 1 chicken fillet (120 g)
- 1/2 plantain
- 2 tbsp guacamole
- 2 cups salad: lettuce, tomato, grated carrot, cucumber

## Method

1. Cook the chicken however you like it, then shred it with two forks.
2. Cut the plantain into thick rounds and cook in the air fryer or oven for 8 minutes.
3. Squash each round flat and return it for another 5 minutes until crisp.
4. Top with the shredded chicken and the guacamole.
5. Serve the salad alongside.

## Notes

Weeks 3 and 11. Baking works if you have no air fryer — the plan explicitly
allows either.
