## Ingredients

- 1 tin tuna, drained
- 1 egg, hard-boiled
- 2 cups lettuce
- 1 carrot, grated
- 1/2 avocado
- 2 slices potato, boiled

## Method

1. Flake the tuna into a bowl.
2. Chop the egg and add it with the grated carrot and lettuce.
3. Fold everything together gently.
4. Serve with the avocado and the potato.

## Notes

Week 12 lunch. Week 14 does the same thing with a cup of rice instead of the
potato.
