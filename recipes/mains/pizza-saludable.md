## Ingredients

- 5 slices aubergine or courgette, or 1 pita bread
- 30 g light mozzarella or fresh cheese
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan.
2. Brush the aubergine or courgette rounds with the oil and roast 8 minutes to drive off water.
3. Top each with tomato, cheese and oregano.
4. Return for 5–7 minutes until the cheese melts.

## Notes

Weeks 4 and 10 dinner. Roasting the rounds first is what stops the pizzas going soggy.
