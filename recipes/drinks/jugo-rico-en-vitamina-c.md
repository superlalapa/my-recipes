## Ingredients

- 1 medium carrot
- 1 orange, juiced
- 1/4 celery stick
- 500 ml water
- 1 cup ice cubes

## Method

1. Blend the carrot, orange juice and celery with a little of the water until smooth.
2. Add the rest of the water and the ice and blend again briefly.
3. Strain if you prefer it thin. Stevia to taste.

## Notes

Weeks 9 and 12. The sheet offers half a cup of potato as an alternative to the
orange juice.
